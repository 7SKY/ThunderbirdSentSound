Choose a WAV, MP3, or PCM AIFF up to 3 MiB. A copy is saved locally; choose it again to update it if the original file changes. AIFF is converted locally to 16-bit PCM WAV; mono/stereo 8–32-bit PCM is supported. Compressed AIFF-C is not supported.

Applies to Send / Send Now in all accounts. No sound for failed sends, drafts, or Send Later. Success means Thunderbird completed sending, not that the recipient received or read the message.

Settings save automatically. Play previews the sound even when automatic playback is disabled.
